### Added

- Change records (roadmap C1): apply detection records every applied workload revision as an immutable change (recommendations, plan, pull-request URL, merge commit, synced revision, workload UID, generations and pod-template SHA-256 digests before and after, rollout start and completion, decision time) with a frozen baseline snapshot of the 7 days before the merge or approval (hourly replicas, requested capacity, trusted allocated cost and components, energy, CO2e, load-driver series, observed unit prices, Impact Model version) and a `content_sha256`. Apply detection also records each workload's pod-template and HPA revisions (`workload_spec_observations`). Migrations 0056 and 0057; change, member, snapshot, measurement and event tables reject `UPDATE` and `DELETE`.
- Change measurement lifecycle (roadmap C2): `applied → measuring → realized | not_realized | inconclusive | contaminated`, `realized → regressed | closed`, with compare-and-swap transitions written with their event; an explicit re-measurement starts a new measurement of a not realized, inconclusive or contaminated change, and a newer change on the workload closes a realized one.
- `GET /api/v2/changes`, `GET /api/v2/changes/{change_id}` and `GET /api/v2/changes/{change_id}/measurements` (roadmap C2).
- Load-driver counterfactual (roadmap C3, MVP): Istio, Linkerd or ingress-nginx requests per second, then the `greenkube.io/load-metric` workload annotation, then CPU usage (flagged weak); hour-of-week matched baseline scaled by the load ratio raised to the quantity's fitted load elasticity; post-window from rollout completion + warm-up, 7 days, aligned coverage of at least 0.8.
- Confounder detection, moving-block bootstrap (one-day blocks) 90% confidence interval and fixed-horizon outcome rule (roadmap C4): another change on the workload, a spec or image change and a recreated workload contaminate the measurement; a change in the baseline, an HPA edit, replica, price-card and instance-type shifts lower its confidence. Estimates are signed and never floored; projection accuracy is reported, not gated.
- One measurement per change (roadmap C5), split across its recommendations by CPU/RAM cost component in proportion to their request reductions (or by projected share), written as per-recommendation `savings_measurements` rows with `change_id`.
- `SavingsMeasurementRepository.get_outcome_counts_by_source_capability()`: realized, not realized and rollback counts per recommendation source and capability, for the decisioning track record.
- Settings `CHANGE_MEASUREMENT_ENABLED`, `CHANGE_MEASUREMENT_PRE_WINDOW_DAYS`, `CHANGE_MEASUREMENT_POST_WINDOW_DAYS`, `CHANGE_MEASUREMENT_WARMUP_HOURS`, `CHANGE_MEASUREMENT_MIN_COVERAGE`, `CHANGE_MEASUREMENT_BOOTSTRAP_SAMPLES`, `CHANGE_MEASUREMENT_EPSILON_RATIO` (Helm `config.recommendations.changeMeasurement.*`).
- Methodology reference `docs/methodology/savings-measurement.md` (roadmap C9).

### Changed

- Recommendations applied by a recorded change are measured by the change-measurement pipeline instead of the interim verifier: they are `verifying` for the 7-day post-window, their health gates run after `VERIFICATION_WINDOW_HOURS` and again at the decision, and they end `verified`, `applied` (`failed` or `inconclusive`) or `rollback_review` as before. Changes recorded earlier, and every change with `CHANGE_MEASUREMENT_ENABLED=false`, keep the interim verifier.
