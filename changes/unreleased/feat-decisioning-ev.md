### Added

- **Expected-value ranking (I1):** every recommendation carries a `value` block in the projection currency, per year: `value_annual` (expected billable cost plus carbon at the carbon price, from the Impact Model projection), `p_success`, `incident_cost` (risk aversion × `RECOMMENDATION_INCIDENT_COST` × severity × blast radius from scope, the `greenkube.io/criticality` label and single replicas), `effort_cost` (engineer hours by effort and delivery mode × `RECOMMENDATION_ENGINEER_HOUR_COST`), `ev = p × value − (1 − p) × incident − effort`, the lane (`savings` or `reliability`, which also counts the incident cost avoided) and every input under `components`. `ranking_score` is the EV and `ranking_factors` break it down in currency (`cost`, `carbon`, `incident_risk`, `effort`, `reliability`), summing to it. The value is persisted in `recommendation_history.value_score` (migration 0058).
- **Profiles as presets and per-request overrides (I2):** `balanced`, `cost_first` (carbon at 0), `carbon_first` (4 × carbon price), `low_risk` (risk aversion 4) and `quick_wins` (effort weight 3, at least 100 per engineer hour). `GET /recommendations`, `/recommendations/active`, `/recommendations/top` and `/recommendations/{id}` accept `?profile=` (`422` when unknown) and `?carbon_price=`; `/recommendations/top` also accepts `metric=value`.
- **Source track record (I3):** `GET /api/v1/sources/track-record` returns a Beta(α, β) entry per source family and capability (uniform prior; `realized` → α + 1, `not_realized` or `rollback_review` → β + 1, `inconclusive` counted only), e.g. `vpa: 14/16 realized`. `p_success` is the Beta posterior of the recommendation's confidence (worth four observations) updated by that record, times a risk-class factor. Scans fold the track record into the persisted score; outcomes come from `get_outcome_counts_by_source_capability()` when the repository offers it, otherwise from the verification state of applied recommendations.
- **Risk model v2 (I4):** per-replica headroom (the hottest replica's p95 against the proposal), OOM and CPU-throttling history, a singleton penalty, HPA (scaling on the changed resource), conflicting VPA writers, blocking PodDisruptionBudgets, a JVM flag (`greenkube.io/jvm: "true"` or `greenkube.io/runtime: jvm`), in-place resize constraints (memory decreases best effort, static CPU manager) and the `greenkube.io/criticality` label, each reported in `risk_factors`. Unobserved facts never change the risk.
- **Server-side eligibility (F10, F7):** every recommendation read through the API carries `eligibility`: `can_open_pr`, `delivery_modes` (`direct`, `manual`), `blockers` and `warnings` with `code`, `message` and `fix`, `eligible_at` while evidence is warming up, `action_kind` and the change set's `recommendation_ids`. It checks the lifecycle status and open pull requests, the action kind and container target, automation configuration, the workload's GitOps binding, the warm-up of usage evidence, and the automation policy through the plan creation's `AutomationPolicyEvaluator`, read-only. Codes are documented in `docs/reference/eligibility-codes.md`.
- Settings `RECOMMENDATION_INCIDENT_COST` (Helm `config.recommendations.incidentCost`, default 100) and `RECOMMENDATION_ENGINEER_HOUR_COST` (`config.recommendations.engineerHourCost`, default 0).
- Frontend: recommendation cards show the expected value and success probability ("≈ €412/yr expected · 82% success"), the top factor with the breakdown in currency, a **Reliability** badge, and the server's blockers with their fix (e.g. "Collecting evidence — eligible in ~31h"). The dashboard's top recommendation cards follow the server ranking and show their expected value.

### Changed

- Recommendations are ordered by expected value: active lists, `/recommendations/top` with a profile, and the dashboard. The recommendations page re-ranks with a profile by reloading the list with `?profile=` instead of re-ranking the top 50 through `/recommendations/top`, so filters and profiles combine.
- The recommendations page decides whether to offer **Preview plan** from the server's `eligibility` instead of about 60 lines of client-side action checks (`safeActionFor`, K2).
- `RECOMMENDATION_RANKING_WEIGHTS` (Helm `config.recommendations.rankingWeights`) is deprecated and ignored, with a warning logged once.

### Removed

- The batch-relative log/min-max normalization of ranking scores and the fixed source authority weights.

### Fixed

- Ranking scores no longer depend on the other recommendations in the batch, so a recommendation's score and relative order no longer change with namespace or other filters (BUG-23).
