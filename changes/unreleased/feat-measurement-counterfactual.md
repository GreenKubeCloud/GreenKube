### Added

- **Load-regression counterfactual (C3 v2).** Change measurement (strategy `request_rightsizing` 2.0) fits `metric = α[slot] + β × load` on the baseline window by ordinary least squares, with hour-of-week slots when the baseline holds two observations of most hours of the week (14 days or more) and weekday/weekend hours of the day otherwise, and predicts each post-window hour from its load. Its 90% interval adds a residual bootstrap of the baseline (one-day blocks, degrees-of-freedom inflation) to the post-window block bootstrap. The fit is used only when its diagnostics pass (enough paired hours and degrees of freedom, identifiable load term, no significantly negative load slope, at most 20% of extrapolated post-window hours); otherwise the quantity falls back to the hour-of-week MVP. Each estimate records its `method` and `counterfactual_params.diagnostics` the fit statistics (R², `β` with its standard error and t-statistic, Durbin-Watson, extrapolated share, failures).
- **Embodied CO₂e measured (C3, component matching).** Hourly buckets and new baseline snapshots carry `embodied_co2e_g` (the embodied emissions the Impact Model allocates by reservation). When both windows carry it, `co2e_g` covers operational plus embodied CO₂e and is compared with the projection of the same components; snapshots frozen earlier keep comparing operational CO₂e with the operational projection, and their digest still verifies. Each CO₂e estimate lists its `components`; `embodied_co2e_g` is reported on its own.
- **Node-pool confounders (C4).** Measurements read the workload's node pools from the node snapshots over both windows: new hardware in the pool (`pool_instance_type_change`) and a pool capacity change above 50% contaminate the measurement; an autoscaler capacity change above 25% (`pool_capacity_change`) and a change to another workload running in the pool during the measurement (`pool_change`) lower its confidence; node churn without a capacity shift (`autoscaler_activity`) is mild. Migration `0073` indexes change events by rollout completion.
- **Drift monitor (C8).** A new `savings-drift` controller loop (hourly, leader only) moves a realized change to `regressed` when at least `CHANGE_DRIFT_THRESHOLD` (0.5) of its per-replica allocated-cost saving is lost on each of the last `CHANGE_DRIFT_SUSTAINED_DAYS` (3) complete days, with the daily evidence in the `regressed` event, and marks its verified recommendations `regressed` (accrual stops). Two hours later it writes one signed `measured` reversal row per recommendation for the accruals since the drift began, once, and records a `savings_reversed` event. A pod template that no longer matches the change, or a recreated workload, closes the realized change (`spec_drift`, `workload_recreated`). Helm `config.recommendations.changeMeasurement.driftThreshold` and `driftSustainedDays`.

### Changed

- **Confounder severities (C4):** an HPA edit after the rollout, a unit-price change above 20% of a resource the change did not touch, and an instance-type change on more than half of the workload's pod-hours now contaminate the measurement instead of only lowering its confidence. An HPA edit inside the baseline window stays moderate.

### Fixed

- **Interim verifier CO₂e compared with the wrong projection:** `core/optimization/verifier.py` compared the measured CO₂e delta (operational CO₂e of the stored rows) with `potential_savings_co2e_grams`, a total that includes embodied emissions the rows never carry, and split the CO₂e of records measured together by their projected cost. It now uses the measurable projection (`measurable_co2e_projection`) and splits by projected measurable CO₂e, as change measurement does. A rightsizing's CO₂e stays reported, not claimed, by the interim verifier.
