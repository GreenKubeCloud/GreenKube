### Added
- **Released capacity, projected (WP-08.1).** Every recommendation read through the API carries `released_capacity` (never persisted, derived from the stored `impact.capacity_released`): annual core-hours, GiB-hours, replica-hours and node-hours (`period: year`), the instantaneous `(current - proposed request) x replicas` in cores and GiB per replica and in total, storage GiB and LoadBalancer count for asset deletions, with units and the `projected` label. The pull-request summary has a "Released capacity (projected)" table, the recommendation list shows a "Frees ... (projected)" line and the detail page a released-capacity block. It is never converted to money and never added to cost savings; increases are negative releases ("Reserves ... more"). `impact.capacity_released` gains `storage_gib` and `load_balancers` (0 on rows stored before).
- **Released capacity, measured, and the capacity verdict (WP-08.1).** The applied detector records per-container requests and limits with each workload spec observation (migration 0140: `workload_spec_observations.container_resources`, `change_measurements.capacity`). Each decided change measurement carries `capacity`: requested cores and GiB per replica before and after (from the specs, not the metric rows), the mean replicas of both windows, the total release at the post-window replicas, core-hours and GiB-hours over the post-window, a separately reported `replica_change` that is never booked as a release, and a verdict `released` / `partially_released` / `not_released` (`unknown` when the requests were not recorded) decided from the specs alone, so it exists when the cost verdict is `inconclusive` and without OpenCost or an autoscaler. Returned by `GET /api/v2/changes/{id}/measurements` and shown on the change page. Measurements decided before keep `capacity: null` and their evidence digest. Not accrued in the savings ledger yet.

### Changed
- **Fixed-capacity clusters: lanes and ranking (IMP-03).** The lane follows the direction of the change: a request increase (or a projection with a negative allocated cost) is in the `reliability` lane, counts the incident cost it avoids and gets the `reliability_change` warning, even when the realization factor 0 makes its billable projection `-0.0`. The ranking value adds `RECOMMENDATION_CAPACITY_VALUE_WEIGHT` (default 1, Helm `config.recommendations.capacityValueWeight`) x (allocated - expected billable), reported as the `released_capacity` EV factor and in `value.components`, so request changes on fixed nodes are ranked by size instead of by risk alone. Recommendations carry `projected_allocated_cost_annual`, `projected_allocated_co2e_annual_g`, `projected_billable_cost_annual` and `projection_note` ("fixed nodes: frees capacity, bill unchanged until nodes are removed"); the recommendation list and the pull-request summary show the allocated figure with that note instead of hiding a 0. Scores are recomputed at every scan and on read: no stored data to migrate, but `ranking_score` values change on every cluster (with weight 1 the value of a request change is its allocated saving).
