### Added
- **Collection coverage (COL-04).** Every collection step is recorded in the new `collection_windows` table (migration 0122): `collected`, `empty` (Prometheus held cAdvisor series for the step but no pod ran), `failed` (reason `no_scrape_data`: no cAdvisor series at all) or `gap` (abandoned before `COLLECTION_MAX_BACKFILL`, or failed until it left it). `GET /api/v1/collection/coverage?start=&end=` returns the steps per status, the coverage ratio and the holes; `GET /api/v1/health` adds the collection watermark, its lag and the last hole; new metrics `greenkube_collection_coverage_ratio{source}` and `greenkube_collection_steps_total{source,status}`. Steps of rows stored before the upgrade are recorded as collected on the first run; other older steps are reported as `unknown`.
- **OpenCost reconciliation (COL-03 step 4 and 5).** Each collected step records the OpenCost allocation total of its window (spread evenly over the window's steps) next to the OpenCost cost its rows carry, and the number of pods stored without a CPU sample; `greenkube_collection_cost_unattributed_ratio{source}` is the share of the last window's OpenCost total that no stored row carries (a warning is logged above 1%). No `__unattributed__` row is written: the missing share is visible in `collection_windows` and in the metric, not added to the totals.
- **Installation identity (COL-02, STO-09).** The controller resolves the `kube-system` namespace UID before collecting (with retries; it does not collect while the UID is unreadable) and stores it once in the new `installation` table (migration 0123). The collection watermark and collection records are keyed by it; watermarks and records written under a cluster or node name are adopted at the first start (the newest watermark wins). A controller whose cluster UID differs from the stored one refuses to collect, unless `config.collection.allowClusterUidChange` (`ALLOW_CLUSTER_UID_CHANGE`) is set.

### Fixed
- A window for which Prometheus returned no series was committed as collected with zero rows, and windows older than the backfill horizon were only counted in a process-local counter: a period with holes looked complete. Such steps are now recorded as failed or gap; failed steps are retried by later runs (one chunk per run) without holding the watermark back (COL-04).
- Containers that lived less than about two scrape intervals (CI steps, short CronJobs) had no CPU sample, since `rate()` needs two scrapes of one series. When a container's CPU counter first appears inside a step, its value over the step length is now stored as its usage (reason `cpu_from_counter_start`): a container that lives 40 s and uses 20 CPU-seconds gets 67 millicores for a 300 s step. Applied only to pods without any rate sample at that step. `CALCULATION_VERSION` becomes `+range.6`; stored `+range.5` rows are not rewritten (COL-03 step 3).
- The auto-detected `CLUSTER_NAME` fell back to the controller's node name when the node list failed at start, so a rescheduled controller started a new watermark and re-collected the whole backfill horizon over existing rows. The fallback is now `default`, the watermark no longer depends on the name, and a missing watermark resumes after the newest stored row instead of re-collecting the horizon (COL-02).
