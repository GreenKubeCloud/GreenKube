### Added
- Chart setting `secrets.ledgerChainKey` (`LEDGER_CHAIN_KEY`), generated once by the credentials bootstrap Job when empty, and metrics `greenkube_ledger_chain_head_keyed{cluster_uid}` and `greenkube_accrual_watermark_ahead_seconds{cluster_name}`, with the chart alerts `GreenKubeAttributionClockSkew` and `GreenKubeAccrualWatermarkAhead` (LED-05, LED-06).
- Migrations 0290 to 0298 (additive): `savings_ledger_entries.chain_key_id` and `annual_rate`, `savings_ledger_totals`, and `row_kind`, `annual_cost_rate`, `annual_co2e_rate`, `currency`, `measurement_id` on `recommendation_savings_ledger`, `head_keyed`, `signature`, `first_keyed_seq`, `key_id` and `key_boundaries` on `ledger_chain_status`, and `ledger_head_acceptances` (with the key boundaries it signed). No stored row or entry is updated: existing entries keep their hash and `chain.ok`.
- `greenkube db ledger-rotate-key --cluster-uid <uid> --reason ...`: records a rotation of the ledger chain key as a sequence boundary signed into the status row and audited in the same transaction, under the append lock (deploy the new Secret first, then run it; without a trusted status row it needs `--trust-entry-keys`, which trusts every configured key over the whole chain and is audited); a previous key then verifies only the entries before it, and a writer whose key is not the one the row names appends nothing (LED-05).
- `greenkube db ledger-accept-head --cluster-uid <uid> --reason ...`: an explicit, logged acceptance of the current ledger head after a restore or failover lost entries, refused when the chain fails the structural checks, keeping the recorded key boundaries, or with `--rebuild-boundaries` moving them later to match the entries (never weaker, never past a boundary the verified head covers, only from a status row signed with a known key, before and after printed), or with `--trust-entry-keys` (only without a trusted row: the recovery for a lost status row) taking them from the entries' own keys, and kept in `ledger_head_acceptances` (migration 0295); it needs `LEDGER_CHAIN_KEY` in its environment (LED-05).

### Fixed
- Ledger keys follow sequence boundaries recorded in the HMAC-signed status row, never time: unkeyed entries are valid only below `first_keyed_seq` (whatever their timestamps), every later entry must use the key of its boundary, and the status row must be signed with the current key. A leaked previous key can no longer forge entries or the status row after a recorded rotation (LED-05).
- With a chain key, the ledger chain status row is signed (migration 0294): an edited row fails verification (`status_forged`), a missing one fails once the chain exists (`status_missing`), and the chart alert `GreenKubeLedgerChainKeyDropped` fires when the head stops being keyed (LED-05).
- The ledger writer fails closed when the head is keyed and `LEDGER_CHAIN_KEY` is missing or different (`greenkube_ledger_write_errors_total{reason="chain_key"}`), so a lost or regenerated Secret can no longer corrupt the chain; `secrets.ledgerChainPreviousKeys` (`LEDGER_CHAIN_PREVIOUS_KEYS`) keeps old keys verify-only for a deliberate rotation (LED-05).
- Savings attribution refuses to run when the process clock is more than 5 minutes from the PostgreSQL clock (`greenkube_attribution_errors_total{reason="clock_skew"}`), never books a slot after the current hour, exposes a watermark left in the future as `greenkube_accrual_watermark_ahead_seconds`, and keeps accruing recommendations while that watermark is ahead (LED-06).
- Each recommendation accrues from its own cursor: a record that was invalid for some runs gets the missed slots back once valid, and a record that accrues again after a pause (a dismissed rollback review, a re-measurement after a refuted measurement) resumes from `baseline.accruing_since`, stamped by the lifecycle, without backfilling the pause (LED-12 c).
- Savings attribution skips a run and counts `clock_check_failed` when the database clock cannot be read, logs the clock-skew error at most every 15 minutes, and the controller warns once when no `LEDGER_CHAIN_KEY` is configured (NOTES says so too). Not done for LED-06: capping accrual at the collection watermark (item 2) and a repair command for rows written in the future (item 3).
- The savings ledger v2 hash chain can be keyed: with `LEDGER_CHAIN_KEY` (generated once by the chart's credentials bootstrap Job, `secrets.ledgerChainKey`) new entries are chained with HMAC-SHA256 and carry `chain_key_id` (migration 0290), so a database writer without the key can no longer multiply values, re-chain and pass verification. Entries already stored keep their sha256 hash and stay valid; verification fails with `hash_mismatch`, `chain_key_missing` or `chain_key_mismatch`, and `greenkube_ledger_chain_head_keyed` publishes whether the head is keyed (LED-05).
- Windowed ledger summaries prorate entries by the same rule as the export's `window_share`, also with only a start or only an end bound (they counted straddling entries in full), and SQLite computes the shares to the microsecond instead of through `julianday` (LED-12 b).
- Drift reversal rows are marked `row_kind = 'drift_reversal'` in the v1 ledger (migration 0291), so a reversal of a saving with mixed signs becomes ledger v2 reversals with reason `drift` instead of two accruals; rows stored before keep the sign rule (LED-12 a).
- A drift reversal cancels the measured accruals actually stored in the reversed interval instead of the measured rate times its duration, so gaps in accrual are no longer over-reversed (LED-12 d).
- The all-time ledger summary, savings report breakdown, lifetime counters and accrued-book reconciliation read the materialized `savings_ledger_totals` (migration 0292, backfilled from the stored entries and updated in the append transaction) instead of scanning every entry on each call; the daily full verification re-derives the totals from the chained entries and fails with `totals_mismatch` when they were edited (LED-09, partial).
- Savings accruals keep their provenance (migration 0293): v1 rows store the annual cost and CO2e rates, the currency and, for measured rows, the measurement read at accrual time; ledger v2 accruals (writer `ledger/4`) carry `annual_rate` in their hash payload, so `value = annual_rate x seconds / 31557600` can be checked from the entry, and a late projection no longer names a later measurement. Entries stored before keep their hash (LED-11, partial: price card and grid intensity are still not filled).
