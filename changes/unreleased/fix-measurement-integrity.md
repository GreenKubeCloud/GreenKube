### Added
- **Savings basis in the API (B6, C2, C7).** `GET /api/v1/recommendations/savings` adds `measured_basis` (`measured_allocated`) and a `realization` block: measured allocated run rate, the realization factor of the measured recommendations' projections and the billed saving it implies (`realized_billed_*_expected`, an expectation; `realized_billed_measured: false`). `GET /api/v2/changes/{id}/measurements` adds `savings_basis` per measurement (`measured_allocated_annual`, `projected_allocated_annual`, `projection_accuracy`, `realization_factor`, `realized_billed_annual_expected`). `GET /api/v2/ledger/summary` adds `currency`, `basis` and `billed` per total, `currencies` and `methods`. Existing fields are unchanged.
- **Deletion rate provenance (H6, C3).** Deletion estimates carry `rate_source`; the measurement's `strategy_version` (`resource_deletion` `1.1:<method>:<rate source>`) becomes the ledger v2 `method_version`.

### Changed
- **Request rightsizing is measured per replica (C3, strategy `request_rightsizing` 2.1).** Every quantity is fitted per replica and multiplied by the post-window replica-hours (`Σ Δrequest × replica-hours`), so a replica count that drops for another reason is never credited to the change. Version 2.2 also fits workload totals and keeps the smaller saving (`counterfactual_params.replica_basis`; `realized` only when both bases are), so a replica rise the change itself caused (an HPA scaling out after a CPU-request cut) is not credited either.
- **Projection accuracy compares with the allocated projection (C4, B).** Change measurements (and the per-recommendation rows) compare the measured allocated saving with the Impact Model's `allocated_cost_annual` and the allocated CO₂e of the measured components, not with the realization-scaled billable projection; projected shares split by allocated projection too.
- **Savings ledger v2 currency (C6).** Each v1 accrual is stamped with its recommendation's projection currency (`impact.currency`) instead of today's configured currency, and the ledger summary keeps one total per method, quantity and currency.

### Fixed
- **Superseded changes accrued forever (C8, BLOCKER).** A realized change closed as `superseded` kept accruing its measured step with nobody watching it. It now stays watched: its accrual ends (`closed → closed`, event `superseded_ended` or `workload_deleted`, with reversal entries in the v1 ledger and `drift` reversals in the ledger v2) when a newer change on the workload ends, or when the workload is deleted or its template drifts while no newer change is realized.
- **Resource deletions booked configured default rates as measured (H6).** A deletion is measured only from the resource's observed trusted cost or a frozen rate with a trusted provenance (OpenCost, Kubecost, pricing catalog); `LOAD_BALANCER_COST_PER_MONTH` / `STORAGE_COST_PER_GIB_MONTH` rates, unknown or mixed provenance leave it `inconclusive` (the saving stays projected).
- **Component split booked the gross CPU saving of a CPU-cut/memory-increase bundle (C5).** Component claims are scaled so they never exceed the change's net measured allocated saving (CPU −$100/yr with memory +$80/yr books $20/yr).
- **Missing or NaN OpenCost cost was a trusted zero (A11, C0.3).** A NaN, infinite or missing `totalCost`/`cpuCost`/`ramCost`, or a zero total for a running pod with requests, is stored with `cost_source = none`; a stored zero total with non-zero requests is not trusted either.
